No account, login, or credentials are required. There are no in-app purchases,
no subscriptions, no user-generated content, and no network connections of any kind.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Health Export reads records the user already has in HealthKit and turns them into
one plain-text document that the user can hand to an AI assistant of their choice.
It reformats existing data. It does not measure, diagnose or interpret anything.

IMPORTANT FOR REVIEW
On a review device HealthKit is usually empty. In that case the app correctly shows
"記録が1件も見つかりませんでした" (No records found) and the Export button stays
disabled. This is expected behaviour, not a malfunction. The app cannot tell the
difference between "no data" and "read access denied" because HealthKit returns an
empty result in both cases, so the message mentions both possibilities.

To see the app working with data, please add a few samples first:
1. Open the Health app, then Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data.
2. Enter a value for two or three different days.
3. Return to Health Export. The metric appears automatically.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Launch the app and tap "はじめる" (Start) on the intro screen.
2. In the HealthKit permission sheet, tap "すべてオンにする" (Turn all on), then "許可" (Allow).
3. Choose one of six purposes. "ふだんの管理" (General) is selected by default and
   includes every metric that has data.
4. Optionally change the period with the - / + buttons (2 weeks to 1 year), or set
   exact dates from "日付で指定".
5. Tap "書き出す" (Export). A sheet appears with the generated text, its character
   and token count, and Copy / Share buttons.
6. "コピー" copies the text to the clipboard. "共有" opens the standard share sheet
   with a .txt file.
7. Detailed settings are reached from "書き出されるデータ" > "項目や形式を変える".

PERMISSIONS
HealthKit read access only. The app never writes to HealthKit. It does not request
clinical health records; the healthkit.access entitlement is an empty array.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. Only Apple frameworks (HealthKit, SwiftUI). All processing happens on device
and nothing is transmitted by the app. What the user does with the exported text
afterwards is their own choice; the privacy policy states this explicitly and
explains how to omit device names, which can contain the owner's name.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Japanese and English are supported, and the exported text can be produced in
either language.

NOT A MEDICAL APP
No diagnosis, no treatment, no health claims. The request text the app attaches for
the AI always ends with a sentence stating that the document is not for medical
diagnosis and that the user will see a doctor if they have symptoms of concern.
