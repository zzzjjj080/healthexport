No account, login, or credentials are required. There are no subscriptions, no
user-generated content, and no network connections of any kind. There is one
optional consumable in-app purchase (a tip), described at the end of these notes.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Health for AI reads records the user already has in HealthKit and turns them into
one plain-text document that the user can hand to an AI assistant of their choice.
It reformats existing data. It does not measure, diagnose or interpret anything.

LANGUAGE ON THE REVIEW DEVICE
The app ships in Japanese and English. A device set to Japanese shows Japanese; a
device set to any other language shows English. On a review device set to English
every label below appears in English, so the English labels are given first.

IMPORTANT FOR REVIEW
On a review device HealthKit is usually empty. In that case the app correctly shows
"No records found" and the Export button stays disabled. This is expected behaviour,
not a malfunction. The app cannot tell the difference between "no data" and "read
access denied" because HealthKit returns an empty result in both cases, so the
message mentions both possibilities.

To see the app working with data, please add a few samples first:
1. Open the Health app, then Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data.
2. Enter a value for two or three different days.
3. Return to Health for AI. The metric appears automatically.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Launch the app and tap "Start" ("はじめる") on the intro screen.
2. In the HealthKit permission sheet, tap "Turn All Categories On", then "Allow".
3. Choose one of six purposes. "General check-in" ("ふだんの管理") is selected by
   default and includes every metric that has data.
4. Optionally change the period with the - / + buttons (2 weeks to 1 year), or set
   exact dates from "Pick dates" ("日付で指定").
5. Tap "Export" ("書き出す"). A sheet appears with the generated text, its character
   and token count, and Copy / Share buttons.
6. "Copy" copies the text to the clipboard. "Share" opens the standard share sheet
   with a .txt file.
7. Detailed settings are reached from "What gets exported" > "Change metrics and format".

PERMISSIONS
HealthKit read access only. The app never writes to HealthKit. It does not request
clinical health records; the healthkit.access entitlement is an empty array.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. Only Apple frameworks (HealthKit, SwiftUI, StoreKit). All processing happens on
device and nothing is transmitted by the app. What the user does with the exported
text afterwards is their own choice; the privacy policy states this explicitly and
explains how to omit device names, which can contain the owner's name.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app requires no network access, so it behaves identically in every region.
Distance and weight can be shown in miles and pounds; the values are read from
HealthKit in those units, not converted afterwards.

NOT A MEDICAL APP
No diagnosis, no treatment, no health claims. The request text the app attaches for
the AI always ends with a sentence stating that the document is not for medical
diagnosis and that the user will see a doctor if they have symptoms of concern.

IN-APP PURCHASE
One consumable, "One coffee", approved with 1.1 and unchanged. It is entirely
optional: nothing is locked behind it and nothing changes after buying. It sits at
the bottom of the detailed settings (Format tab) and as one line under the Copy /
Share buttons after an export.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.3
A localization fix only. In 1.2 a device set to a language other than Japanese or
English (for example French) showed a mixture of Japanese and English, because the
development region did not match the string catalog. Those devices now show the app
entirely in English. No functional change.
