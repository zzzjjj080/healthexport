No account, login, or credentials are required. There are no subscriptions, no
user-generated content, and no network connections of any kind. There is one
optional consumable in-app purchase (a tip), described at the end of these notes.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Health for AI reads records the user already has in HealthKit and turns them into
one plain-text document that the user can hand to an AI assistant of their choice.
It reformats existing data. It does not measure, diagnose or interpret anything.

LANGUAGE ON THE REVIEW DEVICE
The app ships in 12 languages: English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese,
Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Arabic.
It follows the device language; a language without a translation falls back to English.
On a review device set to English every label below appears in English.

Arabic is right-to-left. SwiftUI mirrors the layout automatically; this was verified
in the simulator.

The screenshots are the English ones for every language in this version. Localized
screenshots are planned for a later update. The listing text itself is translated
for all 12 languages.

IMPORTANT FOR REVIEW
On a review device HealthKit is usually empty. In that case the app correctly shows
"No records found" and the Export button stays disabled. This is expected behaviour,
not a malfunction. The app cannot tell the difference between "no data" and "read
access denied" because HealthKit returns an empty result in both cases, so the
message mentions both possibilities.

To see it working with data: Health app > Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data,
enter values for two or three days, then return. The metric appears automatically.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Launch the app and tap "Start" on the intro screen.
2. In the HealthKit permission sheet, tap "Turn All Categories On", then "Allow".
3. Choose one of six purposes. "General check-in" is selected by default and
   includes every metric that has data.
4. Optionally change the period with the - / + buttons (2 weeks to 1 year), or set
   exact dates from "Pick dates".
5. Tap "Export". A sheet appears with the generated text, its character and token
   count, and Copy / Share buttons.
6. "Copy" puts it on the clipboard; "Share" opens the share sheet with a .txt file.
7. Detailed settings: "What gets exported" > "Change metrics or format".

PERMISSIONS
HealthKit read access only. The app never writes to HealthKit. It does not request
clinical health records; the healthkit.access entitlement is an empty array.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. Only Apple frameworks (HealthKit, SwiftUI, StoreKit). Everything happens on
device and nothing is transmitted. The privacy policy states this and explains how to
omit device names, which can contain the owner's name.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. No network access, so it behaves identically in every region. Distance and
weight can be shown in miles and pounds, read from HealthKit in those units.

NOT A MEDICAL APP
No diagnosis, no treatment, no health claims. The request text the app attaches for
the AI always ends with a sentence stating that the document is not for medical
diagnosis and that the user will see a doctor if they have symptoms of concern.

IN-APP PURCHASE
One consumable, "One coffee", approved with 1.1 and unchanged. It is entirely
optional: nothing is locked behind it and nothing changes after buying. It sits at
the bottom of the detailed settings (Format tab) and as one line under the Copy /
Share buttons after an export.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.4
Localization only. No functional change. In 1.2 a device set to a language other than
Japanese or English (for example French) showed a mixture of Japanese and English,
because the development region did not match the string catalog. That is fixed, and
the app is now translated into 12 languages. The exported text can be written in any
of them, including metric names, workout types, sleep stages and mood entries.
